About Me

My name is Gerson Azevedo, I am from Mozambique, and I am an aspiring web developer with a unique background that merges my passion for programming with my studies in General Medicine. My career goal is to work at the intersection of technology and healthcare (HealthTech), leveraging software development to build digital solutions that optimize medical care, enhance patient experiences, and streamline healthcare workflows. I have a strong interest in intuitive interface design, performance optimization, and full-stack architecture. What motivates me daily in web development is the power to transform lines of code into real interactive tools capable of creating a direct, positive impact on society's health and well-being.
